Quick-reference planning notes: winter driving, aurora, hot springs, blue ice cave, shopping and official links.

DRIVING

Winter road basics

Check weather + road conditions more than once a day. Spring can still bring snow, ice and strong wind. Stop only in safe pull-offs.

AURORA

Clouds matter first

Dark sky + clear gaps + aurora activity. With a car, you can self-chase on good nights and keep a tour as backup.

HOT SPRINGS

Blue Lagoon

Do it on arrival day because it sits between KEF and Reykjavík. Pre-book the official day ticket.

BLUE ICE

Guide only

Natural caves change. Go with a glacier operator; the winter meeting point for our current shortlist is Jökulsárlón.

SHOPPING

Souvenir shortlist

Wool, Icelandic design, chocolate, salt, skincare and small local objects. Copenhagen gets its own HAY stop.

MARCH

Pack for layers

Waterproof shell, warm mid-layer, grippy waterproof shoes, gloves, hat and dry socks in the car.
